We've been posting here for a couple of months about [Microsoft's price rises](/2026/08/microsoft-365-price-increase-2026-ai-you-didnt-ask-for/), [the CMA's cloud investigation](/2026/07/ma-cloud-investigation-microsoft-aws-uk-duopoly/) and [what happens when a US company decides you can't use the thing you paid for](/2026/07/anthropic-shutdown-wake-up-call-for-uk-data-sovereignty/). All of it fairly serious. None of it ever stopped to say who we actually are.

So: hello. We're Bigby.cloud 👋

## Who we are

We're Adam and Jamie. Two people, one UK company. Between us we have a long and varied history of technical roles across retail, energy, digital media, fintech and more, with some overlap along the way. We both have years of experience building, running and supporting platforms at scale, for many thousands of users.

## Why we're doing it

The honest answer is that we got fed up.

Not with cloud storage. Having your files follow you between a laptop, a desktop and someone else's computer is one of the genuinely good things about the modern internet. The problem is everything that got bolted onto it.

At some point, keeping your own files safe turned into a relationship where you hold almost none of the cards. The terms of service change and you click accept, because the alternative is losing ten years of photos. The price goes up because an AI assistant you never asked for has been added to your plan, and there is no version of the plan without it. Your files sit on servers owned by a company in another country, subject to that country's politics and that country's courts. And when you finally think about leaving, you find the way out has been made as tedious as possible.

None of that is an accident. It's the business model working as designed. What actually pays for these large tech companies is your attention, your data, and how expensive it is for you to walk away.

We think there's room for something plainer. You pay us money, we store your files. That's the entire arrangement.

## What we're building

[Bigby.drive](/drive/) is our file storage, office and collaboration platform, and it's the thing we're launching first.

Your files are encrypted in transit and at rest on servers in the UK. If you want to work on documents, spreadsheets and presentations without leaving your browser, that's built in, using the excellent open source Collabora project. Shared folders, access control and real time editing are there for teams and groups. It works in a browser and on your desktop, and it speaks WebDAV, so it plugs into tools you probably already use.

The bits that matter to us:

- **We don't look at your files.** No scanning, no analysis, no AI training on anything you store.
- **UK company, UK servers, UK law.** No US parent, so no [US CLOUD Act exposure](/for-uk-data-residency/).
- **You can leave whenever you like.** WebDAV means you can pull everything out with standard tools. No export fees, no hostage-taking.
- **Open source first.** We're built on it, and we [contribute back](https://github.com/bigby-cloud).
- **Honest prices.** Plans start at £3.99 per user per month on annual billing, and you can see [the whole price list](/drive/#pricing). No bloated tiers designed to confuse you into overpaying.

## What we're not doing

There's no free tier. Someone always pays for storage. When it isn't you, it's an advertiser, and the service gets shaped around them rather than around you. We'd rather charge a fair price and be straightforwardly on your side. As the saying goes: “If you're not paying for the product, you are the product.”

We're not an AI company, we're not an advertising company, and we're not a search company. We sell cloud storage, based here in the UK. There's nothing else we'd want your files for.

We have no aspirations to be the next Zuckerberg or Bezos! We just want to build something useful, and to be clear about what your money gets you.
